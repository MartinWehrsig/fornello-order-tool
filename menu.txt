Customisation rules
• Up to five extra toppings per pizza; removing an ingredient is free but does not reduce the
price.
• Half-and-half pizzas are available in Large and Family size. The price is that of the more
expensive half plus €1.00.
• The gluten-free base is prepared in the same kitchen, so we cannot guarantee it is free from
traces of gluten.
• Vegan cheese can replace mozzarella on any pizza for €1.50 (Small), €2.00 (Large) or €3.00
(Family).

Ordering Policies
• Orders can be placed by phone, on our website or through the ordering assistant. Every
order needs a name, phone number and, for delivery, a full address with postal code.
• An order can be changed or cancelled free of charge within 5 minutes of confirmation. After
that it is already in the oven.
• Pre-orders are possible up to 7 days in advance for a chosen time slot during opening hours.
• Large orders of more than 10 pizzas need 24 hours' notice; please call us directly.
• If your delivery is more than 30 minutes later than the time we confirmed, you receive a
€5.00 voucher for your next order.
